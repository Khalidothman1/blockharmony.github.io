Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
01

Introduction

Welcome to Block Harmony. Your privacy is fundamentally important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Quick Summary: Block Harmony is designed as a fully offline game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data whatsoever.

02

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Block Harmony is a completely offline puzzle game that operates entirely on your device.

Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Photos, media, or files from your device
  • Network or internet activity information
  • Financial or payment information
03

Local Data Storage

Block Harmony stores minimal game data locally on your device only to enhance your gaming experience. This data includes:

  • High Score — Your best score is saved locally so you can track your personal progress

This data is stored using your device's local storage mechanism (Shared Preferences). It never leaves your device, is not transmitted to any server, and is not accessible to us or any third party.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

04

Third-Party Services

Block Harmony does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data. This includes:

  • No third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • No advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • No social media integrations
  • No crash reporting services
  • No cloud storage or sync services
05

Internet & Permissions

Block Harmony does not require internet access to function. The game is fully playable offline with no network connectivity needed.

The app requests no special device permissions — no camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or location access is needed or requested.

06

Children's Privacy

Block Harmony is safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we inherently comply with children's privacy protection regulations including:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — Children's provisions
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

No data is collected from any user regardless of age, making the app inherently safe for children.

07

Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breach or unauthorized access to your information through our app.

The locally stored high score data is protected by your device's built-in security mechanisms (device lock, encryption, app sandboxing).

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any material changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and notify users through an app update.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of Block Harmony after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
