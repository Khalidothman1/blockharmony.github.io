BLOCK HARMONY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2025
01

Introduction

Welcome to Block Harmony. Your privacy is fundamentally important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Quick Summary: Block Harmony is a puzzle game that operates primarily on your device. We use Google AdMob to serve ads, which may collect certain device information as described below. We do not collect any personal data ourselves.

02

Information We Collect

We (the developer) do not directly collect any personal information. However, our app uses Google AdMob for advertising, which may collect certain data automatically. See Section 04 for full details.

We personally do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Photos, media, or files from your device
  • Financial or payment information
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
03

Local Data Storage

Block Harmony stores minimal game data locally on your device only to enhance your gaming experience. This includes:

  • Game progress & scores — Saved locally so you can track your personal progress
  • In-app purchase state — Items unlocked via the in-app shop are stored locally
  • Settings & preferences — Your in-game configuration is saved on-device

This data is stored using your device's local storage (Shared Preferences). It never leaves your device, is not transmitted to our servers, and is not accessible to us or any third party (except as noted in Section 04 re: AdMob).

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

04

Third-Party Services & Advertising

Block Harmony uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service operated by Google LLC. By using this app, you agree that AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

What AdMob may collect: Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID), IP address, app usage data, device information (OS version, model), and interaction data with ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time:

  • On Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Or reset your Advertising ID via the same menu
  • Review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy
  • Review AdMob's data practices at support.google.com/admob

We do not control Google's data collection or use practices. Any data collected by AdMob is subject to Google's own Privacy Policy.

05

Internet & Permissions

Block Harmony requires internet access solely to load and display advertisements via Google AdMob. All core gameplay is fully functional offline without network connectivity.

The app requests the following device permissions:

  • INTERNET — Required for AdMob to serve ads
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Allows AdMob to check connectivity before loading ads

No camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or location access is needed or requested.

06

Children's Privacy

Block Harmony is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app uses AdMob for advertising, we have configured AdMob to treat all users in a manner consistent with applicable children's privacy laws where required.

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — Children's provisions
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us at the email below and we will take steps to address the situation.

07

Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal data ourselves, there is no risk of a data breach on our end. The locally stored game data is protected by your device's built-in security mechanisms (device lock, encryption, app sandboxing).

For data collected by Google AdMob, Google employs industry-standard security measures. Please refer to Google's Security Overview for details.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any material changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and notify users through an app update.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of Block Harmony after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
